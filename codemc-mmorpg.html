The MC Project (working title) is a first-person action MMORPG built on a Minecraft server: full-loot PvP zones, a player-driven economy and gear-defined classes in the tradition of Albion Online, with the dungeons, raid bosses and class roles of World of Warcraft. Two things separate it from a typical Minecraft server. Combat is per-bone: every hit is resolved against the target's skeleton, so where you hit matters. And the whole game is written down before it is built — 56 linked design pages that art, numbers and code all read from.

MC 项目（名称未定）是一款在 Minecraft 服务器上运行的第一人称动作 MMORPG：阿尔比恩式的全装备掉落 PVP 分区、玩家驱动的经济与「装备即职业」，加上魔兽世界式的副本、Boss 与职责分工。它和一般 Minecraft 服务器的区别有两点：战斗按骨骼判定，每一击都对着目标骨架结算，打在哪里是有区别的；整个游戏先写清楚再做，56 页互相引用的策划站是美术、数值和代码共同的依据。

Design pillars设计支柱

  • Gear is your class — no character level. Weapons give Q / E / R, the off-hand gives F, armour pieces give T / Y / G; mastery lines level by use.装备即职业：没有角色等级。武器给 Q / E / R，副手给 F，防具给 T / Y / G；专精线靠使用升级。
  • Where you hit matters — per-bone hit boxes with part multipliers and effect gauges: stun a head, disarm an arm, break a shield.打在哪里有区别：逐骨骼碰撞箱、部位倍率和积累条：打头眩晕、打臂卸力、打盾破盾。
  • Risk is a map — four zone tiers (safe / contested / plunder / lawless) decide what you lose on death and what the land produces.风险画在地图上：四档分区（安全 / 纷争 / 掠夺 / 无法）决定死亡掉什么、土地产什么。
  • Players make the economy — almost every item is player-crafted; regional markets do not connect; taxes are the gold sink.经济由玩家制造：几乎所有物品由玩家制造；地区集市互不连通；税费是金币回收口。
  • Dungeons teach, bosses test — every wild-zone monster teaches one mechanic; each dungeon boss is the exam for that chapter.副本教学，Boss 验收：野外每种怪教一个机制，每本 Boss 就是该章的考卷。

Hit the part, not the box打部位，不打盒子

Vanilla Minecraft gives every entity one bounding box. Here each entity has per-part hit boxes bound to its pose; melee swings sweep a capsule from hand to blade tip along the weapon's keyframed arc, and the first part it touches is the part you hit. Each part carries a damage multiplier and an effect gauge: hits fill the gauge, the gauge triggers the effect, then the part is immune for a window and its threshold grows, so a long fight settles at roughly one trigger per eight hits.

原版 Minecraft 每个实体只有一个碰撞盒。这里每个实体有绑定姿势的部位碰撞箱；近战挥砍时，从手到刀尖的胶囊体沿武器关键帧轨迹扫掠，碰到的第一个部位就是命中部位。每个部位有伤害倍率和效果积累条：命中累积，积满触发效果，然后进入免疫窗并抬高阈值，长战斗大约每 8 击触发一次。

How it is implemented: TeaEngine实现：TeaEngine

TeaEngine is the team's own engine mod for the game — Fabric 1.21.1, Java 21 — and it owns combat end to end. Hit boxes are written into the model itself: a Blockbench group named _box:head is the head's collision box, so the artist who builds the model also defines what can be hit. The client sweeps those boxes frame by frame against the pose the player actually sees and reports which part it touched; the server checks the claim against a rest-pose envelope, the animation's contact window and range, and only then applies damage. The rule written into the design: the client measures, the server rules — and anti-cheat never costs game feel.

TeaEngine 是团队自研的游戏引擎模组（Fabric 1.21.1，Java 21），战斗从头到尾由它负责。碰撞盒直接写在模型里：Blockbench 里名为 _box:head 的组就是头部碰撞盒，做模型的美术同时定义了什么能被打到。客户端对玩家真正看到的姿势逐帧扫掠这些盒子，上报打到了哪个部位；服务端用静止位姿包络、动画的判定窗口和射程复核，通过才结算伤害。写进设计里的规则是：客户端量，服务端裁——反作弊不为它牺牲手感。

  • Three layers, one authority — the Fabric server is the only game-logic authority and settles everything in memory within the tick; the client has zero authority; the Spring Boot backend owns persistent truth and cross-instance coordination and is never on a combat or movement call path.三层，一个权威：Fabric 服务端是唯一的游戏逻辑权威，tick 内内存结算；客户端零权威；Spring Boot 后端只管持久真相和跨实例协调，绝不出现在战斗或移动的调用链上。
  • Skills are chains of atoms — a skill is a list of atoms with only two triggers, OnStart and OnSignal(name); wind-up and recovery are explicit Wait atoms, so new timings need no engine change. 18 atom types; the schema is validated at load and any error rejects the whole file rather than shipping a half-working skill.技能是原子链：一条技能就是一串原子，触发只有 OnStart 和 OnSignal(名字)，前后摇是显式的 Wait，加新时机不用改引擎。18 种原子；schema 在加载期校验，任一条不过整份不上，不让半套技能上线。
  • Models straight from Blockbench — .bbmodel is converted to Bedrock geometry and animation and fed to a loader that has been validated in-game; one merged model is split into first- and third-person variants at pack time by tag, so artists maintain a single file.模型直接来自 Blockbench：.bbmodel 转成 Bedrock 几何和动画，喂给已实机验证的加载器；一份合并源模型在打包时按标签拆成第一 / 第三人称两份，美术只维护一个文件。
  • Rig pose and root frame kept apart — the same clip is placed twice, once in camera space to draw and once at (eye position, facing) to judge; what you see is what is judged, with no separate reach knob.绑定位姿与 root 帧分离：同一份 clip 摆两次，一次在相机空间画，一次在（眼睛位置，朝向）判；看到的就是判定的，不留额外的 reach 旋钮。
  • Debug is a first-class feature — a hit-box overlay shows the boxes appearing and vanishing mid-swing, the fastest way to check a contact window; per-part readouts print on hit.调试是正式功能：判定盒叠加层能看到盒子在挥击中途出现又消失，是确认判定窗口最快的办法；命中时打印部位读数。

04Classes & Growth职业与成长

Classes are mastery lines named after what the weapon does, not a choice made at character creation. Seven lines are specified, four ship first. Each line is tuned on seven dimensions where 100% is baseline; the numbers are coefficients, so every later value — skill multipliers, ranges, cooldowns, control durations — derives from base × coefficient.

职业是按武器命名的专精线，不是建号时的选择。定了 7 条线，首批 4 条。每条线在 7 个维度上定档，100% 为基准；数字就是系数，之后所有数值（倍率、射程、冷却、控制时长）都按「基准 × 系数」推导。

08Procedural · R1随机地牢 · R1

R1, the Cataclysm Rift, is the generated counterpart to the hand-built dungeons: every entry is a new map stitched from room templates by seed. Rifts open at random wild spots, are not on the map, and vanish if nobody enters within 30 minutes. A thin crack is a solo rift — one linear expedition; a wide maw is a trio rift — a 3×3 grid per floor with keystone doors, shortcuts and revisitable floors. The tier comes from the zone the entrance is in.

R1 灾厄裂隙是手工副本的生成版：每次进入都是按种子从房间模板拼出来的新地图。裂隙在野外随机位置出现、不上地图、30 分钟无人进入就消失。细缝是 1 人本，线型远征；巨口是 3 人本，每层九宫格，带钥石门、捷径和可回访的楼层。T 阶由入口所在区决定。
